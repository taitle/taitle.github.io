About

Hey, I’m Ramazan. I’ve been tinkering with technology and hacking since I was a kid, and this is my corner of the internet where I share some of that.

I was born and raised in Turkey and went to college there, with a semester in California along the way. My first security job was an internship at NATO in Belgium, doing penetration testing. I then started my career in Amsterdam as a consultant in banking, pentesting web applications, before moving on to malware analysis and reverse engineering.

Since 2021 I’ve been doing detection and incident response at Atlassian. In 2024 I moved to New York, where I’m based now, still at Atlassian.

You can check out my rants on twitter or contact me via LinkedIn.
